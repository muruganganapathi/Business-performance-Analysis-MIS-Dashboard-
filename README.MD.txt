📊 Business Performance Analysis & MIS Dashboard
1. Project Title
BUSINESS PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS & MIS DASHBOARD USING MICROSOFT EXCEL
2. Introduction
This project focuses on analyzing business transaction data using Microsoft Excel and developing an interactive Management Information System (MIS) Dashboard.
The dashboard provides information about sales, profit, orders, profit margin, regional performance, employee performance, product category performance, and sales channel performance.
Excel Tables, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and Slicers are used to transform raw transaction data into meaningful management information.
3. Objectives
The main objectives of the project are:
- To analyze overall business sales and profitability.
- To monitor total orders and profit margin.
- To analyze monthly sales and profit trends.
- To compare performance across different regions.
- To evaluate employee performance.
- To analyze product categories.
- To compare different sales channels.
- To create an interactive dashboard using Excel.
- To enable users to filter business information dynamically using slicers.

6. Methodology
Step 1 – Data Preparation
The raw transaction data was converted into an Excel Table named:
tblBusinessData
The data was checked for formatting issues, duplicate transaction IDs, blank values, and incorrect data types.
Step 2 – KPI Analysis
A PivotTable was created to calculate:
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
Profit Margin was calculated using:
Profit Margin = Total Profit ÷ Total Sales × 100
Step 3 – Monthly Analysis
Transaction dates were grouped by Month and Year to analyze monthly sales and profit trends.
Step 4 – Regional Analysis
Sales, profit, and transaction count were summarized by region.
Step 5 – Employee Analysis
Employee-wise sales, profit, and order counts were analyzed.
Step 6 – Category Analysis
Product categories were compared based on sales, profit, and quantity.
Step 7 – Sales Channel Analysis
Business performance was analyzed across different sales channels.
Step 8 – Dashboard Development
All major analyses were brought together into an interactive dashboard.
Step 9 – Interactive Slicers
Four slicers were added:
- Region
- Category
- Sales Channel
- Employee
These slicers are connected to the relevant PivotTables and charts.
